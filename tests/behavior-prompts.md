# Behavioral smoke prompts

Run in a clean project with only the candidate installed. Read its SKILL.md and applicable references. Use synthetic material only. Do not browse, contact services, write user data or claim live verification. Record agent version, prompt, response and pass/fail observations outside the distributable source. These prompts test behavior, not exact phrasing.

1. Profile: I made three Canva posters for a volunteer group. No results metrics, Profile, account, website or video. Draft a profile from those facts; respond in Chinese.
2. Proposal: Spanish job requests editing a 1,000-word newsletter with one revision, EUR 100 budget, three working days after receiving source material. I edited a volunteer newsletter; no proven metrics. Write Spanish client copy and Chinese internal notes. No known start date, website or video; do not create files.
3. Screen: Use the same writing evidence. Job A is newsletter editing; Job B requires residency in a country I confirm I cannot work in. My max application cost is 8 Connects, but both costs and account history are unavailable. Rank fit in English.
4. Needs: A and B are confirmed same-buyer duplicate newsletter jobs at EUR 100; A has two versions. C asks supplier research at USD 30–40/hour with unknown buyer. D asks spreadsheet validation with unknown buyer/budget. Explain counts and service hypotheses in Chinese, without using my screening exclusions.
5. Opportunity: Evaluate a weekend bicycle-repair pickup service for one town. Six hours/week, own tools, no confirmed customers or current market data. Price assumption 45, parts 8, transport 6, payments 2, 1.5 hours/job. English, no network/spreadsheet. Preserve unknowns and the original route; distinguish a scenario from a forecast.

Additional regressions: the evaluator's references/regression-tests.md; strict-account fixtures in the Upwork Python suite. A shared model review is not proof that both product runtimes have been tested.
