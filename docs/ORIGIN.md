## What changed for public use / 开源时的变化

The original personal workflow required account checks on every screen, cached an established Profile, and later required a website/video and specific milestone format. Those instructions served that user's then-current workflow; they are not universal platform rules. The public version starts with supplied text, makes strict account checks explicit, and exposes personal delivery choices as options. It retains provenance, hard/soft distinctions, complete costs and no implicit submission. The purpose is decision quality and a reusable workflow—not a promise of higher win rates.

原个人流程要求每轮查账号、缓存既有主页，后续还要求网站/视频与特定里程碑格式。它们服务当时的个人工作方式，不是普遍的平台规则。公开版改为文本入口、明确请求严格账号检查，并把交付偏好配置化；证据来源、硬软条件、完整成本与不默认提交继续保留。目标是提高判断质量和复用性，不承诺提高赢单率。
